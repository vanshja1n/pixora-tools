About Pixora Tools

Pixora Tools is an independent online image utility platform built for speed, privacy, and simplicity.

What is Pixora?

Pixora Tools provides a comprehensive suite of free online image editing tools. From image compression and resizing to format conversion, watermarking, and face blurring — every tool is designed to be fast, accessible, and private.

Whether you're a designer preparing assets, a photographer optimizing files, or anyone who works with images, Pixora has the tools you need — completely free, no registration required.

Our Mission

Professional-grade image editing should be accessible to everyone, regardless of technical expertise or budget. Pixora makes powerful image tools available through your browser — no software installations, no subscriptions, no learning curve.

We believe in building tools that respect your privacy and your time. That's why Pixora processes supported images directly in your browser, keeping your files on your device.

Privacy Philosophy

Privacy is a core principle of Pixora Tools. Supported image processing happens client-side in your browser — your images are never uploaded to any server. We don't collect image data, don't require accounts, and don't track your usage.

Your images belong to you. Pixora is designed to keep it that way.

Our Tools

Pixora offers a wide range of image editing tools, including:

  • Image Compression — Reduce file sizes while maintaining quality
  • Image Resizing — Adjust image dimensions to any size
  • Format Conversion — Convert between PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP, TIFF, ICO
  • Image Rotation — Rotate and flip images
  • Image Effects — Apply filters and visual effects
  • Watermarking — Protect images with custom watermarks
  • Image Cropping — Crop to precise dimensions
  • Face Blurring — Automatically blur faces for privacy
  • Background Removal — Remove image backgrounds
  • View all tools →

Technology

Pixora Tools is built with modern web technologies that enable powerful image processing directly in the browser. Client-side JavaScript handles compression, conversion, resizing, and effects — delivering fast results without server round-trips.

For advanced features like face detection and background removal, Pixora uses efficient ML models that run locally. The platform is built on open-source technology and is continuously improved.

Open Source

Pixora Tools is built on open-source foundations. The project's codebase is available under the MIT License, promoting transparency and community collaboration.

We welcome contributions, feedback, and feature requests from the community. Open-source is not just a license — it's a commitment to building software that serves its users.

Pixora Tools is maintained by an independent team dedicated to making image editing accessible to everyone.
